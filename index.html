02 Working method

Less dashboard.
More judgment.

The method is part of the output: it shows where the claim came from, what it can change, and where it stops.

01

Frame the decision

What would change if this number moved? Define the audience and decision before selecting the chart.

↗
02

Interrogate the evidence

Use segmentation, rates over raw counts, and model metrics that match the cost of being wrong.

↗
03

Make the caveat visible

Confidence includes knowing what the dataset cannot explain and what the next investigation should add.
