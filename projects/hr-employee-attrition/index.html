03 Analytical readout

Three signals worth carrying forward.

The useful output is not “who will leave.” It is which operating questions should be investigated next.

01 / WORKLOAD

Overtime is the sharpest observed split.

Attrition is 30.5% with overtime versus 10.4% without. The next test should distinguish workload, schedule, and role mix.

02 / TENURE

Early tenure deserves a first look.

Employees in the 0–2 year band show 34.9% observed attrition, compared with 10.4% for 10+ years.

03 / MODEL

Accuracy is not the only success metric.

Logistic Regression records 56% recall versus Random Forest at 21%, while Random Forest reports higher accuracy. The cost of missed leavers matters.

PEOPLE ANALYTICS RECOMMENDATION

Use the model to prioritize conversations, not automate a verdict.

The dataset can identify segments for further investigation; it cannot explain individual intent or replace manager context.

  1. Pair overtime and early-tenure flags with manager and engagement context.
  2. Use recall when missing a potential leaver is more costly than reviewing extra cases.
  3. Re-test the model on current, organic HR data before operational use.

LIMITATION

Association is not causation.

Exit interviews, manager notes, engagement surveys, and current HR data would be needed to explain why employees leave.
